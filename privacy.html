Praxis Chat privacy

Praxis Chat runs on your PC. Overmind Labs does not collect, receive, or store your data.

  • Your chats, projects, files, memories and settings are stored only on your PC, in %APPDATA%\PraxisChat.
  • When you send a message, the prompt goes only to the model provider you connected, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • API keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager.
  • The app checks GitHub for new versions. That check sends nothing about you or your data, beyond what any download sends (your IP address and app version).
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts.

Questions: open an issue on the releases page.

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
